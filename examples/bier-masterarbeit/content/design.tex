\chapter{Experimental Design}
\label{cha:design}

\section{Brews}
Twelve identical brews of a pale ale (original gravity 12.5\,\textdegree{}P) were split into four
temperature levels with three replicates each (Table~\ref{tab:recipe}).

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Recipe and fermentation parameters (per 20\,L brew)}
  \label{tab:recipe}
  \begin{tabular}{lrl}
    \toprule
    Ingredient / parameter & Amount & Unit \\
    \midrule
    Pale ale malt          & 4.2      & kg \\
    Cascade hops           & 60       & g \\
    Top-fermenting yeast   & 11.5     & g \\
    Fermentation temperature & 16--24 & \textdegree{}C \\
    Fermentation time      & 7        & days \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\section{Analysis and Tasting}
Esters were measured by gas chromatography. In addition, 24 participants rated the beers blind on
fruitiness, cleanness and overall impression (scale 1--9).
