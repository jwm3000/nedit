\chapter{Brewing Trials}
\label{cha:implementation}

\section{Procedure}
All brews were mashed, boiled and hopped identically on a 50\,L pilot plant. After
pitching, each fermenter was kept at its target temperature with a deviation of at most
0.5\,K.

\section{Sampling}
Samples were taken daily; the final analysis was performed after seven days of fermentation.
