# Vorlagen / Templates

`masterarbeit/` is copied into every new nEdit project.

- **Thesis** (`main.tex`, `template/`, `content/`, `figures/`): LaTeX KOMA template by
  Karl Voit et al. (<https://github.com/novoid/LaTeX-KOMA-template>), TU Graz title page by
  Stefan Kroboth and Karl Voit – licensed under **CC BY-SA 3.0**
  (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>). Content replaced with placeholders.
- **Presentation** (`praesentation/`): TU Graz Beamer theme 2018 by Maria Eichlseder, adapted by
  Michael Krisper and Yavuz Koroglu; design based on the TU Graz template by Christina Fraueneder
  (<https://latex.tugraz.at/vorlagen/tugraz>). nEdit change: default (empty) institute URL so the
  theme also works without an institute option.
- Logos and corporate design belong to Graz University of Technology. nEdit is a private project
  and not affiliated with TU Graz.
